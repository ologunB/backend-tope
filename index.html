About

The work has been remote nearly the whole time: a migration platform run out of Canada, a family social app out of Israel, an ed-tech platform serving students across Africa, and a cross-border payments product before those. The thread running through them is distance. Matching people to jobs abroad, helping families nearby find each other, moving money between currencies.

In practice that means REST and WebSocket APIs in Node.js, Python and PHP, PostgreSQL and PostGIS for anything geospatial, Redis for caching, BullMQ for background work, Flutterwave for payments, and LLM search and matching running in production rather than in a demo. I tend to own a feature from schema through to deploy, and I'm usually the one on call for it afterwards.

I studied electrical and electronic engineering before any of this, which is probably why I still think about systems in terms of load, tolerance and what happens at the edges.

Work

2021 —

Blumefy

Software Developer  ·  Remote, Canada

Blumefy helps African professionals find visa-sponsored jobs abroad and settle once they arrive. I lead backend development for around a thousand active users. The part I'm most invested in is the Job Bank and its matching service, which uses LLM semantic search to rank sponsored roles against a candidate's real profile instead of keyword-matching a CV. I built the REST APIs, real-time sync and authentication on Flask and Firebase, and I own deployment, query performance and uptime across both environments.

Python  ·  Flask  ·  Firebase  ·  LLM semantic search  ·  REST

Backend Engineer  ·  Remote

A fair amount of time also went into making the interface behave the same way across fifty-odd Android and iOS device configurations.
